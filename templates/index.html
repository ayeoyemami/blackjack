Blackjack RL

Reinforcement learning meets the casino

🃏 How to Play

  • You and the dealer are each dealt two cards. The dealer's first card is face‑down.
  • Cards 2–10 are worth their number. Face cards (J, Q, K) are worth 10. Aces are worth 1 or 11.
  • Hit to draw another card. Stand to keep your hand.
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.
  • Going over 21 is a bust - instant loss.
  • The dealer must draw until their hand is at least 17.

🤖 The RL Agents

  • Two algorithms were trained to play: Q-Learning and SARSA.
  • Both explore the game state space - defined by the player's score, the dealer's upcard, and whether an Ace is high.
  • Q-Learning (off-policy) learns the best possible action regardless of how it explores.
  • SARSA (on-policy) learns from the actions it actually takes while exploring.
  • Each agent was trained for 10 000 episodes and evaluated on 5 000 games.
